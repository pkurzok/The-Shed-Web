# Privacy Policy

<p class="meta">Last updated: 24 October 2026 · <a href="/de/privacy/" lang="de" hreflang="de">Deutsche Fassung</a></p>

This policy covers the app The Shed for iPad and Mac and this website, shed.peterkurzok.de. The app is made by Peter
Kurzok ("we", see [Contact](#contact)).

## In short

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking.
- Your songs never leave your device. We run no server that could receive them.

## Your songs

Songs you import into The Shed (from the Files app, or by drag and drop on the Mac) are copied into the app's storage
on your device. The analysis (sections, bars, tempo, key), the stems and the chords are computed on the device and
stored next to the song. Deleting a song deletes them; deleting the app deletes everything. We never receive your
songs or anything computed from them.

## Model downloads

On first use, The Shed downloads its separation model (about 425 MB) once from Hugging Face. If you have The Shed Pro
and choose "Best quality", the app downloads a second model (about 701 MB) at that moment. Like any server, Hugging
Face sees the IP address of your device and the files requested; no song and no identifier of yours is sent. Hugging
Face's handling of this data is described in its [privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

The chord recognition is part of the app and needs no download.

## Purchases

The Shed Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never see your
name, address or payment details. The app only learns from the App Store whether Pro is active on the device. See
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support e-mail

When you write to us, for example from the feedback row in the app's settings, the e-mail is sent by your own mail
app. We use
your address and your message only to answer you, and delete them when the matter is done.

## No analytics, no tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code and does not track you across apps or websites.
Crash reports that you share with developers in your device's settings reach us through Apple, without any
information that identifies you.

## This website

This website consists of static pages served by Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads
nothing from other servers; its fonts are served from this domain. To deliver the pages and protect them against
attacks, Cloudflare processes the IP address of your device; see
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Children

The Shed collects no personal data, from children or anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If the app or this website changes how data is handled, we update this policy and the date at the top.
